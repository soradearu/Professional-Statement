# Professional-Statement

I am an aspiring cyber security professional. I enjoy understanding how systems work, how they break and trying to find ways to make them more secure. I let my curiosity guide me through this never-ending learning path that is cyber security. 

I don’t have a degree in IT,  honestly I didn’t even finish my own major (which was Korean Language And Literature) simply because I didn’t find it interesting and fullfiling enough. In my last year just I was about to hand in my thesis, and some other classes, I found a different path, I went into development. I took classes, worked for hours, still was lacking but I started to apply. I worked as a front-end developer for a couple of months even did some freelance jobs. 

But it’s like Gloria Estefan said: “**Rhythm is gonna get you.” I discovered a path that I know it’s going to the hardest but also the most satisfying: Cyber security. A never ending chaos in the midst of a world that keeps changing. This also meant that I will never stop learning which is my ultimate goal in life.**

Ever since I started this path, I researched, absorbed and created applications that helps me retain the information. I made a Linux terminal inspired website first -which is also my portfolio website-, so that I can understand how things worked underneath and also my friend would learn how to navigate through Linux. I also contributed to the community through CTF write-ups, walkthroughs. I will keep creating and writing as long as I learn.  

I’ll never forget the first time that I’ve felt that spark, though. A finishing projects for one of the courses I attended was to chose a topic and do a presentation on it. I chose Cyber Threat Intelligence and of course my topic was Solarwinds Attack. I quite enjoy researching but this was different in a way that got me excited to the point of euphoria. There was so much I didn’t understand. Isn’t that amazing?

Since then, I’ve been hands-on with CTFs, web security, and Linux environments, bridging my development background with threat analysis and incident triage. I’m looking for a team where that continuous learning never stops, with the long-term goal of growing into a technical security leader.

I’d also like to approach cyber security issues from a psychological perspective. I try to see the situations through the lens of the attacker first, then analyse the systems and calculate the risk. I think as well as having system knowledge, knowing attacker’ motivations are important - not only for social engineering attacks. 

With all of this curiosity of mine and all of seemingly unconventional methods of learning, I want to be able protect myself, others and system to the best of my abilities. One day I analyse a malware and try to understand their perspective even if it’s beyond ethical(by my morals), other day I go and learn about Active Directory. I think my biggest strength is not that I can learn and adapt fast but I never stand still and try to do my best at everything and also my cool-headedness in any kind of situation which is a skill I gained from life.
